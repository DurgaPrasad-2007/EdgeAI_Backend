# References and Parts Adapted

- **Open-RMF:** Adapted its fleet-adapter separation, task handling and traffic-coordination architecture. https://github.com/open-rmf/rmf
- **ORCA paper:** Adapted the reciprocal local decision and safe-yielding principle for collision avoidance. https://gamma.cs.unc.edu/ORCA/publications/ORCA.pdf
- **MAPF paper:** Used its vertex/edge conflict definitions and makespan metric for modelling and evaluation. https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.08291
- **DC-MRTA paper:** Adapted decentralized task bidding, reassignment and navigation during aisle blockage. https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.02865
- **Open-RMF Demos:** Referenced its multi-robot warehouse simulation and fleet-visualization approach. https://github.com/open-rmf/rmf_demos

**Our implementation:** We combined these ideas into our own peer-to-peer intent and expiring-corridor-lease system for three AMRs. No source code or published results were copied.
